# FIRE TAIL 0.9.7

- Fixed initiative positions; the active turn highlight moves without rotating the queue. Smaller cards adapt to the available width.
- Compact battle controls with health immediately above movement and attack buttons.
- Larger dice and pulsing movement numbers after rolls settle.
- Glass item notifications with a yellow stripe on the right and a soft fade to the left.
- Includes the 0.9.6 cooperative battle, chat, character, terrain and menu improvements.

Validation: 144 automated tests, TypeScript checks and production build passed. Cooperative battles were checked with two local clients. Internet play, reconnection and full cross-scene synchronization still require further validation.
